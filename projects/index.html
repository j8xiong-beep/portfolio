Spotify Popularity Analysis

Data Science · Machine Learning · Python

This project explores what makes a song popular on Spotify using a dataset of more than 114,000 songs. I analyzed audio features such as danceability, energy, loudness, acousticness, and valence to understand how they relate to song popularity.

I performed exploratory data analysis, permutation hypothesis tests, missingness analysis, feature engineering, and predictive modeling. I also compared a baseline regression model with a Ridge Regression model using additional audio and categorical features.

The final model improved the RMSE from 22.24 to 19.14. The analysis also showed that danceability had a statistically significant but relatively small relationship with popularity, suggesting that song popularity depends on many factors beyond individual audio features.

Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention

Data Analysis · E-Commerce · Statistical Analysis

This project investigates which online browsing behaviors are associated with whether a shopper completes a purchase during an online shopping session.

Using the UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset, we analyzed 12,330 shopping sessions and focused on variables including the number of product-related pages viewed, time spent on product pages, bounce rate, exit rate, and visitor type.

Our exploratory analysis found that purchase sessions viewed more product-related pages and spent more time on those pages. Purchase sessions also had lower average bounce rates and exit rates compared with non-purchase sessions.

The project also proposed statistical hypothesis testing and a logistic regression model to examine whether browsing behavior can help predict purchase outcomes while accounting for the strong class imbalance in the dataset.
